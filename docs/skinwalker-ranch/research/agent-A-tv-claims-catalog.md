## Section 2 — LiDAR / subsurface

### CLAIM-03 — LiDAR "wormhole" (the scan with a hole in it)
- **Episode:** **S4E11** per Metabunk's index; an S4E14 finale clip is titled "Lidar Wormhole – Meeting With Brandon." Original reveal episode **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **As broadcast:** drone-borne LiDAR produced a point cloud showing a **red annulus with a black void at its centre**, presented as a possible **wormhole / dimensional aperture**.
- **Raw data:** no `.las`/`.laz` point cloud released. Not peer-reviewed. Not replicated.
- **Counter-analysis (Metabunk 13038):** the void is **nadir occlusion** — the unscanned cone directly beneath a stationary sensor; the drone flew **well below the sensor's recommended 50–100 m altitude**; a proper serpentine/orbital pattern reproduces the terrain with **no hole and no artifacts**.

## Section 4 — Recovered material / "superconductor"

### CLAIM-11 — Cross-hatch patterned ceramic recovered from inside the mesa
- **Episode:** **S6E1 "Bubble Trouble."** Discovery dated to shortly after **10:00 pm, 25 September 2024**.
- **As broadcast:** ceramic pieces bearing a **cross-hatch / geometric grid pattern**, characterised as **man-made / manufactured**.
- **Who:** **archaeologist Chris Roberts** called the team out after his crew found the material.
- **Raw data:** no chain-of-custody documentation, stratigraphic context, or independent sampling released.

### CLAIM-12 — "Self-healing" ceramic under SEM
- **Episode:** Season 6 lab segment; clips reference **S6E11**. Episode number moderately confident, unverified.
- **As broadcast:** under SEM the ceramic's **porous surface enlarged its holes and changed structure** under the electron beam; when the microscope was **switched off and back on, the material appeared to have returned to its original state**. The SEM also confirmed the grid pattern, which the lab physicist reportedly said **appeared tool-carved**.
- **Who:** **Dr. Brian Patchett**, physicist at **Utah Valley University**; Taylor, Bard, Fugal present.
- **Reported composition:** SEM/EDS surface returned **carbon, oxygen, silicon, magnesium, aluminium**. **XRF** returned **nickel, iron, cobalt**; a hand magnet showed strong attraction.
- **Raw data:** no micrographs, EDS spectra, or XRF output published for independent review. Metabunk thread 14382 analyses the SEM claim.
- **Note:** C/O/Si/Mg/Al is an unremarkable silicate/aluminosilicate signature; Fe/Ni/Co is a common magnetic-alloy signature. **The elements and the interpretation must be assessed separately.**

### CLAIM-13 — Superconductivity / Meissner-effect levitation
- **Episode:** Season 6 cryogenic lab segment. **Episode UNCONFIRMED.**
- **Version A (as broadcast):** the sample in **liquid nitrogen at −320 °F** displayed **anomalous heat capacity**, **repelled an underlying magnetic field**, and achieved **stable levitation** — the classic **Meissner effect**, characterised as a **high-temperature superconductor**.
- **⚠ INTERNAL CONTRADICTION — Version B:** an independent summary describes the **opposite outcome** — that the liquid nitrogen **continued to boil violently for an anomalously long time**, indicating the material **resisted temperature change** and **prevented the Meissner test from being completed**.
- These cannot both describe the same segment. Version A appears to originate from an **AI-generated SEO site** (`dealxtop.com`); Version B from **episode-recap sources**. **Unresolved.** Provisionally, treat "confirmed levitating superconductor" as **NOT ESTABLISHED even at the level of what was broadcast.**
- Version B is itself an on-air acknowledgement that **the test did not complete** — a partial in-episode caveat.

### CLAIM-14 — "Art's Parts" (contextual; NOT a ranch-recovered sample)
- Included because the user flagged it as adjacent. **Not a ranch dig find.**
- **Claim:** layered **magnesium (shiny side) and bismuth (dark side)**; magnesium is a **Mg-Zn alloy at ~2.4% zinc**. A **90 mg sample in distilled water** reportedly showed **magnesium ions at 60× normal concentration** and **hydrogen bubbles after 40 days**.
- **This one has genuine external analysis, and it is negative.** **Isotopic analysis indicated terrestrial magnesium.** A published skeptical analysis argues the layering is **non-substrate overspray accumulation from a magnetron sputtering chamber** using high-purity Bi and Mg/Zn targets in argon plasma — ordinary industrial thin-film deposition waste.

## Section 7 — Radiation and human health

### CLAIM-20 — Dangerous EM/microwave radiation atop the mesa
- **Episode:** **S1E1 "Bad Things Happen When You Dig"** (31 March 2020).
- **As broadcast:** EM radiation at levels **described as dangerous to humans**, stated in **milliwatts per square metre (mW/m²)** — a **non-ionizing power-flux-density** unit, **not mR/hr**. Characterised as far stronger than a cell phone or Wi-Fi router, with the assertion that "microwaves like that don't exist in nature." **Taylor reported feeling "swimmy-headed."** Ranch manager **Jim Morse's iPhone** was said to have "developed a mind of its own."
- **Genuine on-air caveat:** the episode itself raised **aboveground nuclear testing at the Nevada Test Site (1945–early 1950s)** depositing fallout carried over the Uinta Basin. Note this addresses *ionizing* contamination, not the *microwave* reading.

### CLAIM-21 — Gamma spikes at 511 keV (the annihilation line)
- **Episode:** recurring; **UNCONFIRMED**. Also asserted by Taylor in interviews.
- **Claim:** gamma spikes measured with a **calibrated gamma spectrometer** showing a peak at **511 keV**. Taylor's stated inference: this occurs "nowhere except in particle colliders," and its localised occurrence implies technology not known to exist.
- **Raw data:** **no spectra released.** *This is the highest-value target for data release — a 511 keV photopeak is trivially verifiable from a single saved spectrum file.*
- **Note:** 511 keV is emitted by **many ordinary β+ emitters**, appears in **background from cosmic-ray secondaries**, and in **medical PET isotopes**. The "only in colliders" framing is what needs adversarial attention, not the measurement.

### CLAIM-22 — Travis Taylor's cistern dosimeter event
- **Episode:** associated with **Homestead 2**; one summary places it in Season 1, another is non-committal. **SEASON/EPISODE UNCONFIRMED — do not assert Season 1.**
- **As broadcast:** Taylor **removed the cover from an outdoor cistern**, became dizzy, and team **dosimeters recorded a heavy dose of ionizing radiation** with the **alarm triggering**. Taylor subsequently reported **classic symptoms of radiation poisoning**, and separately reports **nightmares** since filming.
- **⚠ Significant gap:** **no dose figures in mR, mrem, mSv, or µSv/h were recoverable from any source.** **The specific mR/hr numbers requested could not be confirmed — not one.**

### CLAIM-23 — Thomas Winterton's head injuries
- **Episode:** maps to **S1E3 "High Strangeness"** (21 April 2020). Winterton is said to have had **three unexplained head injuries**; the others' episodes **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **As broadcast:** Winterton began **digging near Homestead 2 after ignoring warnings**, developed a severe headache, sought care. Reported progression: **large goose-egg swellings**, eventually his **scalp separating from his skull**. Doctors reportedly **pierced it and drained the fluid** and were **unable to explain the cause**.
- **Note:** a subgaleal hematoma with scalp-plane separation is a **recognised, mundane consequence of blunt head trauma**; the anomaly claim rests on the absence of a *remembered* injury.

### CLAIM-24 — General illness pattern
- Nausea/nosebleeds/headaches are commonly attributed to the ranch in general discourse, but **no specific broadcast nosebleed incident tied to a named individual or episode could be confirmed.**
- **Dragon (Bryant Arnold)** was **absent from Season 6**; reporting phrased as "He's been doing better," implying a health reason. **Nature of the condition and whether the show attributed it to the ranch is UNCONFIRMED.**

## Source quality notes

### Series structure
Premiered **31 March 2020**; **73 episodes** as of **19 May 2026** per one summary. S1: 8 eps. S2: 10. S3: 11. S4: 15. S5: 14. S6: 14. S7: 14, from 19 May 2026. **These totals imply 86, not 73 — internally inconsistent and unreconciled. Treat season lengths as approximate.**

### Attested ONLY by the show / ranch-affiliated parties
**No independent raw data, no peer review, no replication:** CLAIMS 01–13, 15–18, 20–25, 28–35, 37–39. **That is 34 of 39 claims.**

The uniform pattern across all instrument-based claims: **no raw files of any kind have ever been released** — no LiDAR point clouds, no gamma spectra, no SDR IQ captures, no drone telemetry, no drill logs, no SEM micrographs, no necropsy report, no dosimeter readouts.

Every physical claim rests on **broadcast depiction alone**. Where independent analysis exists at all, it is **hostile to the anomalous interpretation** (Art's Parts isotopes; LiDAR nadir occlusion; satellite behind cloud; rolling shutter).

**Most tractable independent verification targets, in order:** (1) **the 1.6 GHz band survey** — anyone with an SDR; (2) **the 1964–69 aerial photo gap** — public USGS/USDA archives; (3) **the 511 keV gamma spectrum** — a single saved file would settle it.
