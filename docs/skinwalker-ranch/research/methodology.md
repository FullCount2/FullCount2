# Scoring methodology (draft)

## The central move: split "did it happen?" from "is it anomalous?"

Nearly every dispute about Skinwalker Ranch collapses because two different
questions get answered with one number. This document scores them separately.

**Axis 1 — Occurrence confidence (0–100):** How confident are we that the
physical event happened roughly as reported? "A drill bit broke." "A Geiger
counter displayed 2 mR/hr." "A drone lost telemetry and descended."

**Axis 2 — Anomaly confidence (0–100):** Conditional on the event having
occurred, how confident are we that it *cannot* be accounted for by known
physics, instrument behavior, environmental conditions, base rates, or
ordinary human factors?

A claim can be ~95 on Axis 1 and ~3 on Axis 2. That is not a contradiction;
it is the single most common shape in this dataset. High Axis-1 / low Axis-2
is what "true but unremarkable" looks like.

The product (A1 × A2) is the only number that should move anyone's prior
about the ranch. Report it as **Net Anomaly Weight**.

## Axis 3 — Evidence Grade (A–F)

| Grade | Standard |
|---|---|
| **A** | Peer-reviewed publication; raw data public; independently replicated |
| **B** | Raw data public; reviewed by independent qualified experts; not peer-reviewed |
| **C** | Multiple independent firsthand witnesses **plus** instrument records, but all data held by an interested party |
| **D** | Broadcast depiction only; instrument shown on screen; no raw data; witnesses are financially or reputationally interested |
| **E** | Single-witness anecdote, published after the fact, no instrument record |
| **F** | Second- or third-hand; uncorroborated; or contradicted by a better source |

Note: essentially the entire corpus lives at **D and E**. That is itself the
most important finding in this report, and it is a fact about the evidence,
not a slur on the witnesses.

## Rules of engagement for the adversarial process

1. **Absence of evidence is weak evidence of absence — but withheld evidence
   from a party that controls it and profits from ambiguity is stronger.**
   If you filmed the instrument, you have the data. Not releasing it after
   six seasons is informative.
2. **Debunks must clear the same bar as claims.** "Probably swamp gas" with
   no calculation is not superior to "probably a portal" with no calculation.
   Weak skeptical explanations get flagged as weak.
3. **Base rates before exotica.** Before asking whether a drone was downed by
   an unknown field, establish how often that drone model fails per flight hour.
4. **Selection effects are the default hypothesis for any "cluster."** Dozens
   of instruments run continuously; only excursions are broadcast.
5. **Credentials establish competence in a domain, not correctness in an
   instance.** A real PhD operating outside their specialty with no controls
   is not authority-grade evidence.
6. **Steelman before scoring.** Every claim gets its strongest advocate before
   it gets its score.
7. **Say when something doesn't add up.** Where no mundane explanation is
   adequate, the report must say so plainly rather than manufacture a debunk.
