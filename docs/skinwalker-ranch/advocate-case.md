Calliphorid flies require roughly 10 °C for flight and oviposition, and larval development
effectively halts below that. **Central Oregon in late February does not provide it.** The insect
leg of the model — which agent D identifies as "the direct source of 'clean, straight-edged'
removals" — is simply unavailable. Agent D says so plainly and to its credit.

**Three additional features that are not addressed by the residual explanations:**

1. **The temporal cluster.** Six carcasses in seven days in one county is far above background adult

3. **The observers are the right observers.** These are not television personalities with a
   franchise. They are law-enforcement officers with no commercial stake, examining carcasses in

**What this legitimately supports — and it is narrower than it looks:**

   parcel* inside a region that was simultaneously producing reports. It cannot be used — as it
   frequently is — to argue that nothing was happening in the Uinta Basin.

**Now the question that matters: could a 60-year null coexist with genuine intermittent phenomena,
and what would the phenomena have to be like?** This is the right question and the honest answer
constrains me severely.

For compatibility, the phenomena must satisfy **at least one** of:

- **Low rate combined with a high interpretive threshold.** If events occur a few times per decade
  and are individually unremarkable to a working rancher — lights in the sky in a basin where lights
  in the sky are ordinary; the occasional dead cow on an operation that loses 1–4% of adult stock
  annually — then a family not *looking* for anomalies classifies every one as ordinary. This
  requires nothing exotic and it is probably the single most important observation in this document:
  **the difference between the Myers period and the Sherman period may be an interpretive frame, not
  a physical one.** The Shermans were newcomers arriving into a basin with forty years of accumulated
  UFO lore and a local clearinghouse (Hicks) ready to receive their report. That is a
  **reinterpretation** model. It fits the record far better than either "the location activated" or
  "the family fabricated," and it is not seriously considered anywhere in the file.
- **Onset.** Skeptics treat coincidence-with-arrival as damning, and it is the single most suspicious
  fact in the entire subject. But **every terrestrial-agency hypothesis predicts onset**: a
  transmitter installed; oilfield development intensifying; a human actor beginning to operate; the
  RF environment of 1994 bearing no resemblance to that of 1934. The Myers null is **decisive against
  "this location has an intrinsic, timeless property"** — which is the mythological claim, and I
  concede it. It is **much weaker against "something started in the 1990s."**

**And the constraint this places on me, stated plainly.** Combine the Myers null with the modern
record: seven seasons, continuous instrumentation, **64 live cameras on a paid subscription
service**, and agent E's viewer observation that season after season produces "the same pea-sized
images of shimmering objects with no new information." If a photographable phenomenon occurred at
any appreciable rate on 512 acres under that coverage, we would have it by now. **We do not.**

Therefore whatever is or is not happening is **rare, brief, non-optical, non-local, or nonexistent.**
That is a narrow hypothesis space and I accept being confined to it. It rules out most of what the
show implies. It does not rule out an intermittent terrestrial actor, and it does not rule out
nothing.

## 10. The historical base rate on dismissing intermittent field reports — used carefully

This is the argument every fringe advocate reaches for, and it is usually abused. I will state it
with its counter-base-rate attached.

Phenomena reported by field observers and dismissed by physicists as observer error, later confirmed
by instruments: **meteorites** (the Académie des Sciences position into the 1790s); **rogue waves**
(sailors' anecdotes until the Draupner platform laser altimeter recorded a 25.6 m wave on 1 January
1995); **sprites and blue jets** (aircrew reports dismissed for ~70 years, first instrumentally
captured in 1989 and confirmed by dedicated aircraft campaigns in 1993–94); **ball lightning** (still
without a settled mechanism, but with a 2014 spectroscopic capture); **gamma-ray bursts** (as above).
*(These are my own knowledge, not in the files.)*

**The counter-base-rate, which is much larger and which I am obliged to state:** N-rays, polywater,
cold fusion, the Martian canals, EmDrive, and — agent D's own excellent example — **LK-99, which went
from levitation video to a Cu₂S impurity explanation in under two months.** For every ball lightning
there are a hundred N-rays. The prior on "unexplained field report resolves prosaically" is
overwhelming and I do not dispute it.

**So the argument is not "therefore this is real."** It is narrower and it is sound:

1. In every case where dismissal turned out to be wrong, **the resolution came from a deliberate
   instrumented campaign with controls, not from argument.** Sprites were settled by one aircraft
   campaign. Rogue waves by one platform-mounted altimeter.
2. Those campaigns were **cheap relative to the money already spent arguing.**
3. **And here is the disanalogy I must concede, and it is the strongest argument against my own
   position:** in every confirmed case, the pre-instrumental reports were *consistent* — multiple
   independent trained observers describing the **same specific morphology**. Sprites were always
   red, always above thunderstorms, always brief. Rogue waves were always a single steep wall.
   **The Skinwalker corpus has no signature.** It is lights, plus a bulletproof wolf, plus incinerated
   dogs, plus a portal humanoid, plus drill bits, plus RF, plus cattle, plus equipment failure, plus
   nightmares that follow people home. A real phenomenon usually has a morphology. **A heterogeneous
   grab-bag is the signature of narrative accretion around a location, not of a physical cause** —
   and that is the strongest single reason to doubt everything I have written above.

I state this against myself because the exercise is worthless otherwise. It is why my case is about
**one or two specific, narrow, terrestrially-shaped residuals** and about **methodological
unfairness**, and not about the property.

18. **The show cannot function as scientific evidence, and that is the producers' fault.**
    Broadcast-only, edit-controlled, subject-as-executive-producer, no raw data of any kind released
    in six years, an undisclosed $5M conflict with the analyzing university, and a trademark for
    "motion pictures and television shows" filed in **February 2017 — three years before** the owner's
    "zero intention to monetize it in any way." Conceded entirely.
19. **The look-elsewhere effect invalidates every unpre-registered "spike."** Ten detectors at 1 s
    integration over one 8-hour night yields roughly **780 three-sigma excursions from noise alone**.
    Reporting one as "the anomaly" is guaranteed output, not weak evidence. Conceded, and it is the
    single most important quantitative point in the entire file.
20. **The 511 keV inference is false.** Annihilation radiation at 511 keV is ubiquitous in outdoor
    gamma spectra from cosmic-ray secondaries and from ordinary β⁺ emitters. "This occurs nowhere
    except in particle colliders" is wrong. The *measurement* may well be real; the *interpretation*
    is not defensible.
21. **Credential and title inflation is real.** Taylor holds two earned doctorates, not four, and is
    an engineer rather than a research astrophysicist; the "Chief Scientist of the UAP Task Force"
    title materially overstates an informal, part-time, time-limited assignment. Erik Bard's physics
    degree **could not be independently verified from any source**, and "plasma physicist" is
    unsupported by his patent record — while he holds the title of Chief Scientist. Conceded.

## The strongest argument against my own position

22. **The corpus has no signature.** Every historical case where dismissal of field reports turned out
    to be wrong involved *consistent* reports — trained observers independently describing the same
    specific morphology. Sprites were always red, always above thunderstorms, always milliseconds.
    Rogue waves were always a single steep wall. **This corpus is lights, plus a bulletproof wolf,
    plus incinerated dogs, plus a portal humanoid, plus drill bits, plus RF, plus radiation, plus
    cattle, plus equipment failure, plus nightmares that follow people home.** That heterogeneity is
    the signature of narrative accretion around a location, not of a physical cause. It is the
    strongest single reason to doubt everything in Part 1, and I do not have a good answer to it.

## Closing

The honest summary of thirty years is not "investigated and found wanting." It is **"never
investigated."** Two well-funded programmes and a television series have generated books, episodes,
merchandise, a federal contract and a great deal of argument, and have produced **not one controlled
measurement**. Their critics — correct on nearly every specific — have analyzed a sample chosen for
them by a production company and have left the one free public-archive check undone.

I concede almost the entire corpus. What survives is small, terrestrially-shaped, and specific: an
unidentified emitter that at least one competent analysis implies must exist; a possible spoofing
event whose raw logs are sitting on a hard drive; a cluster of cold-weather cattle deaths that the
standard biological model cannot mechanically cover; a regional sighting record forty years older
than the mythology whose obvious explanation nobody has ever actually made; and a Stanford
immunologist's judgment that a state actor injured someone in connection with that property.

None of that is a portal. Some of it is checkable this month for less than the cost of a single
episode. **The correct verdict on Skinwalker Ranch is not "debunked" and not "proven." It is
"untested" — and after thirty years and tens of millions of dollars, that is a far more damning
finding about everyone involved than either side has been willing to state.**
